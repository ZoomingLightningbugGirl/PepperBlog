    • About Habanero

    Habaneros (haw-buh-NAYR-oh) are chili peppers with a spice range of 100,000 to 350,000 scoville heat units.

    History

    The habanero is thought to have originated in Meso- or South-America.

    Taxonomy

    Class: Eudicots

    Order: Solanalese

    Family: Solanaceae

    Genus: Capsicum

    Species: Capsicum chinense
